# STEP 07 — Lighting & Mood

**Purpose**  
Improve lighting and atmosphere without changing content.

**Input / Output**

```
[Scene_Master]
      ↓
Lighting_Master_v1
```

**Prompt**

```
# Goal

Improve lighting and mood only.

# Preserve

Keep unchanged:
- person
- face
- body
- pose
- outfit
- scene

# Apply

Professional fashion lighting:
- soft key light
- subtle fill light
- natural rim light
- realistic shadows

Match:
- lighting direction
- color temperature
- existing scene atmosphere

Preserve natural skin tone under the new lighting.

# Mood

Elegant.
Confident.
Sophisticated.
Editorial fashion atmosphere.

# Quality

Cinematic color grading.
Natural skin texture.
Photorealistic.

Save:
Lighting_Master_v1
```
